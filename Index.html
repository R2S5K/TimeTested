Time Tested

Time Tested is an action role-playing game currently in development. Take control of different animal characters, each with their own abilities, personalities, and stories, and fight your way through a world filled with dangerous enemies and mysterious locations. Time Tested combines dark fantasy, action RPG combat, exploration, and a story centered around animals fighting back against the people who changed their lives forever. The game is still being developed, and this website follows the progress of the project, including its characters, world, story, artwork, and the work that goes into bringing the game to life.
