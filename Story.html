Story Overview

Time Tested takes place in a dark fantasy world where animals have been subjected to experimentation and changed in ways that gave them strange and powerful abilities. The animals must survive in a world that was never meant to be theirs while uncovering the truth behind what was done to them. As they explore the world, they encounter dangerous creatures, unfamiliar environments, and the people connected to the experiments. Their journey is about survival, discovery, and finding out what happened to the animals that came before them.

Sovereign Apothecary Network (S.A.N.)

The Sovereign Apothecary Network, known as SAN, is an organization with influence over laboratories, zoos, water treatment facilities, and other parts of society. Behind these operations is a much larger system of experimentation involving animals. The experiments conducted by SAN have changed animals in ways that gave them unusual abilities. The consequences of these experiments are a major part of the world of Time Tested and the reason many of the animals are now fighting back.

The Animals

The animals affected directly or indirectly by S.A.N.'s experiments are at the core of Time Tested. Each playable animal has been affected in a different way, giving them unique abilities and their own reasons for seeking the truth behind these experiments. Some have been physically altered, while others have been shaped by the cruelty and consequences of S.A.N.'s actions. Every animal has fallen victim to some form of animal testing or cruelty, and their individual experiences shape how they see the world and the atrocities committed against them. As they begin to uncover the truth, they must confront not only the people responsible, but also the lasting effects of what was done to them.
