About the Developer

My name is Ryan Kibbler and I am a Creative Technology and Design student at Arizona State University with a passion for game development, programming, 3D modeling, and digital art. I enjoy combining technical skills with visual design to create interactive experiences. I am currently developing my skills in Unity, Blender, C#, and other digital media tools while working toward creating my own games and interactive projects.

Games that Inspire Me

Several games have influenced the way I think about game design and what I want to create. Games such as Path of Exile 2, Dark Souls, Jak and Daxter, and Baldur's Gate 3 have inspired different aspects of my approach to gameplay, exploration, combat, and world design. These games have helped shape the ideas and direction behind my own projects.

My Approach to Game Development

I am interested in creating games that combine strong visual design with engaging gameplay. I enjoy experimenting with different tools and techniques, learning through each project, and gradually turning ideas into playable experiences. Time Tested is one of the projects I am using to develop my skills and explore what I can create as a game developer by combining everything that I love into one game.
